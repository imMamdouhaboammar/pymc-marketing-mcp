# RFC 002: Risk-Aware Optimization — Lower-Quantile Value-at-Risk Utility Objective

- **Status**: Accepted / Implemented
- **Date**: 2026-09-19
- **Target seam**: `src/marketing_mcp/domain/decisions/utility.py`

## Motivation & Problem Statement

In marketing budget allocation, standard optimization defaults to maximizing expected response ($E[Y]$) or expected net profit ($E[\Pi]$). While risk-neutral, this treats high-uncertainty channels (wide posterior credible intervals) identically to well-identified channels (tight credible intervals) with the same mean response.

Marketing executives and risk-sensitive planners require risk-aware optimization that explicitly penalizes estimation uncertainty and downside tail risk without replacing Bayesian posterior distributions with arbitrary point confidence scores.

## Mathematical Specification

### Objective: `lower_quantile`

For $S$ posterior response draws $y^{(s)} \sim p(Y \mid \text{data}, \text{spend})$ and financial assumptions:
- $m = \text{effective\_margin\_rate}$
- $r = \text{revenue\_per\_outcome}$
- $C = \text{spend}$

The posterior net profit draws are:

$$\Pi^{(s)} = y^{(s)} \cdot r \cdot m - C$$

The scalar utility at quantile level $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ (default $\alpha = 0.10$, representing the 10th percentile / Value-at-Risk guarantee) is:

$$\text{Utility} = \text{Quantile}_\alpha(\{\Pi^{(s)}\}_{s=1}^S)$$

### Invariants & Properties

1. **Units**: Identical to currency / net profit (e.g. dollars).
2. **Downside Risk Sensitivity**: When two allocation options have the same expected net profit $E[\Pi]$, the option with lower posterior variance has a higher 10th percentile and is preferred by the optimizer.
3. **Point-Estimate Consistency**: When posterior draws have zero variance ($y^{(s)} = \bar{y}$ for all $s$), $\text{Quantile}_\alpha(\Pi) = E[\Pi]$ for any $\alpha \in (0, 1)$.
4. **Shift Invariance**: Adding a fixed profit increment $+\Delta$ shifts utility by exactly $+\Delta$.
5. **Spend Linearity**: Increasing spend by $+c$ reduces utility by exactly $-c$ holding response fixed.

## Decision Gate Safety

1. **Server-Side Gate Prerequisite**: Downstream optimization cannot be invoked on rejected models. The existing `_approved(model_id)` check in `DecisionService` strictly blocks models that have unaddressed divergences, $R\text{-hat} > 1.05$, or bulk $\text{ESS} < 50$.
2. **Zero Response Reimplementation**: The objective consumes posterior draws generated by the PyMC-Marketing model path; it does not implement separate response equations.
3. **Decision Provenance**: Optimization results record the objective metadata:
   ```json
   {
     "objective": "lower_quantile",
     "quantile": 0.10,
     "description": "Lower 10% quantile of posterior net profit (Value-at-Risk guarantee)"
   }
   ```
