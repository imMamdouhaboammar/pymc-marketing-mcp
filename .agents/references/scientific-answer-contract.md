# Scientific Answer Contract

Use this contract for substantive analytical answers produced from PyMC Marketing MCP outputs. Simple operational replies such as job status do not need every section.

## Required structure

**Question addressed** — restate the analytical question without broadening it.

**Data/model used** — include dataset ID, model ID, and lineage when calibration or model ancestry matters.

**Model health** — report the persisted diagnostic decision status and material warnings. Never hide `approved_with_caution` or treat `approved` as causal proof.

**Evidence** — name the server-returned posterior/predictive quantity, uncertainty interval or distribution summary, and comparison evidence relevant to the question.

**Interpretation** — state only what those tool outputs support. Separate contribution, predictive performance, prior robustness, and experimental/causal evidence.

**Limitations** — surface identification assumptions, prior sensitivity, extrapolation, data limitations, and unavailable checks that materially affect the conclusion.

**Decision implication** — include only when the current model’s decision gate permits the requested decision operation. Do not bypass a rejected gate with manual arithmetic.

**Next measurement** — include when uncertainty could materially change the decision and a server-returned measurement recommendation or a clearly identified experiment would reduce it.

## Authority rules

Model-dependent numbers come only from PyMC-Marketing/PyMC/ArviZ-backed MCP tool outputs or persisted artifacts generated by those libraries. Never fabricate channel contribution, ROAS/iROAS, posterior means, intervals, diagnostics, model-comparison values, probability alive, expected spend, or CLV.

If authoritative output is missing, stale, failed, or blocked, say so and stop at the last supported claim. Preserve provenance, warnings, maturity (`stable`, `experimental`, `deprecated`), and server decision status.

## Audience

When the reader is a marketing or business stakeholder, keep every section above and change the order: lead with the decision implication and its confidence, then the evidence, then limitations and the next measurement, and keep model IDs and sampler metrics at the end. The [marketing decision playbook](marketing://skills/references/marketing-decision-playbook) covers vocabulary (platform ROAS, total and marginal iROAS, lift) and phrasing. Reordering never removes a warning, an interval, or a caution status.
