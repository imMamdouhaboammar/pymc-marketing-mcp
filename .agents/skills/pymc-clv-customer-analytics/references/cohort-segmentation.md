# Customer Cohort Segmentation & Retention Playbook

Translate Bayesian CLV and $P(\text{alive})$ metrics into CRM and retention marketing actions.

---

## 1. Customer Value Matrix

- **Champions ($P(\text{alive}) > 0.80$, High Expected CLV)**:
  - Protect and reward with loyalty benefits.
- **At-Risk VIPs ($P(\text{alive}) \in [0.20, 0.50]$, High Historical Spend)**:
  - Immediate retention outreach and win-back offers.
- **Lost / Dormant ($P(\text{alive}) < 0.10$)**:
  - Suppress from expensive paid retargeting lists to eliminate wasted ad spend.
