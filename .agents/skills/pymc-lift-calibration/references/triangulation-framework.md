# Incrementality Triangulation Framework

Triangulation unites observational MMM, multi-touch attribution (MTA), and randomized experiments into a cohesive decision framework.

---

## 1. The Attribution Hierarchy

| Methodology | Primary Strength | Structural Weakness | Role in Marketing Science |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Multi-Touch Attribution (MTA)** | Real-time user touchpoints | Ignores offline, over-credits brand search | Short-term tactical creative rotation. |
| **Observational MMM** | Holistic, privacy-safe macro view | Prone to endogeneity bias | Macro budget allocation across quarters. |
| **Randomized Experiments (Lift)** | Gold standard causal truth | Expensive, localized point-in-time | Likelihood anchors in Bayesian MMM calibration. |

---

## 2. Resolving Conflicting Signals

When MTA reports Brand Search ROAS $= 8.0$, Observational MMM reports $3.5$, and a Geo-Holdout Test shows $1.2$:
1. **Root Cause**: Brand search captures existing high-intent demand generated by TV and social branding.
2. **Action**: Calibrate the MMM with the $1.2$ holdout result (`calibrate_mmm`).
3. **Result**: The posterior saturation curve shifts down, preventing the budget optimizer from over-allocating capital to captive brand search demand.
